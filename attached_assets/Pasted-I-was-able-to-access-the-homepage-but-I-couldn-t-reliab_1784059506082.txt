One thing I would change from your current announcement system

Right now your announcement page feels like an announcement.

I would make it feel like a digital memorial.

That means:

* larger typography
* more breathing room
* fewer boxes
* portrait becomes the hero
* obituary becomes the centerpiece

The service details become supporting information rather than the visual focus.

Implementation Plan

I’d build this in four phases:

Phase 1 (1–2 days)

* Add a Remember (or Memorials) navigation item.
* Create a memorial index page with search, recent memorials, and archive.
* Pull published announcements into this page automatically.

Phase 2 (2–3 days)

* Redesign the memorial detail page using your current announcement layout as the foundation, with improved typography, spacing, and gallery support.
* Add a “Share” action and printable service information.

Phase 3 (2–4 days)

* Extend the staff portal:
    * Draft → Review → Published workflow
    * Preview before publishing
    * Schedule publication
    * Archive management

Phase 4 (future enhancement)

* Family collaboration portal
* Guestbook moderation
* Photo uploads
* Tribute videos
* Memorial donations or charity links (optional)

I actually think this becomes a competitive advantage. Most funeral home sites feel either dated or overly commercial. If Norwert Hills treats memorials with the same restraint and attention to detail as the rest of the brand, families won’t feel like they’ve left the website—they’ll feel like they’ve entered a quieter, more personal space within it. That aligns well with the premium, understated identity you’ve been building.
